Troubleshooting: What to Do If a Coupon Fails

If your coupon code does not apply as expected, check these four common reasons:

⚠️ Accidental Whitespace

When copying codes, ensure you didn't accidentally include a leading or trailing space before or after the code characters.

🔤 Case Sensitivity

Many promotional codes require capital letters (for example, typing DISCOUNT10 instead of discount10).

🏷️ Plan Eligibility

Certain codes are exclusive to specific tiers (e.g., promotional codes created specifically for 6-Month or Lifetime licenses).

⏳ Campaign Expiration

Seasonal holiday or event codes are time-sensitive. If a campaign has passed its closing date, the system will notify you.

Can I use more than one coupon code on a single purchase?

FXnod allows one verified promo coupon per checkout order. The system will always apply the highest eligible discount for your selected plan.

Can I apply a coupon code on the mobile app?

Yes, 100%! The FXnod Mobile App includes the exact same interactive Plan Purchase Sheet, complete with coupon validation and the 15-minute price lock timer.

What happens if I don't have enough wallet balance?

If your wallet balance is lower than the discounted total, the checkout sheet will show a gentle alert with a 1-tap shortcut to "Top Up Wallet". You can deposit the needed balance and return to complete your order within your 15-minute lock window.

Every completed order generates an itemized digital receipt showing your original price, coupon discount deducted, and final amount paid. This receipt is permanently stored under the Wallet & Orders tab.
