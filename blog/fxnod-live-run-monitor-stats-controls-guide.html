Let's break down each visual component so you know exactly what each gauge represents:

📊 1. Key Metrics Grid

At the top of your screen, four instant statistics summarize your active session:
• Session Net P&L: Glowing green for profits, soft red for drawdowns.
• Total Runs: Number of completed trade cycles.
• Wins vs. Losses: Exact count of wins and losses.
• Current Win Rate: Real-time mathematical percentage.

🎯 2. Take-Profit Progress Meter

Fills up smoothly with every winning contract, displaying your exact percentage distance to your session target. Once it hits 100%, the engine automatically ends the run and locks in your gains. (Compare models: Fixed Plan vs Own Amount).

Visualizes your remaining safety buffer before your pre-set Stop-Loss ceiling is reached. As long as this bar remains healthy, your session is operating well within calculated mathematical risk boundaries.

👻 4. The Shadow Gauge

For phantom trading bots, this gauge monitors background ticks. Clearly indicates whether the bot is in Shadow Mode (evaluating ticks with $0 risk) or Active Lane (placing real trades). Learn more: What is the Shadow Lane?

What Happens When a Bot Finishes Its Run?

An automated bot should never run forever. FXnod provides two automated termination scenarios that prevent uncontrolled exposure:

Scenario A: Take-Profit Achieved 🎉

The moment your session profits cross your pre-set target (e.g., reaching $5.00 profit on a $50 Plan):

  • The Live Monitor flashes a celebratory "Take-Profit Reached" badge.
  • The engine stops placing orders automatically.
  • Your session is finalized and logged under Ended Runs.
  • You can close the app with your profits safely banked!

Scenario B: Stop-Loss Ceiling Reached 🛡️

If a challenging market streak reaches your pre-set safety boundary:

  • The bot immediately shuts down to honor your Stop-Loss.
  • The Refused Stake rule prevents oversized emergency orders.
  • Your remaining account balance is preserved, completely preventing an account wipeout.

4 Best Practices for Supervising Live Bots

To get the most out of the Live Run Monitor, adhere to these professional trading habits:

1. Don't Stare at Every Tick

Algorithmic trading is designed to free your mind. Glancing at the progress meter occasionally is healthy; obsessing over every 1-second tick causes unnecessary anxiety.

2. Trust Planned Recovery Steps

If a bot experiences a single loss on a planned recovery ladder, let the pre-calculated math work. Avoid hitting Emergency Stop prematurely during normal variance.

3. Stop on External Disruptions

If your Wi-Fi restarts or your mobile signal drops to 1 bar, use the Force Stop button to pause your session until your connection is stable.

4. Review Ended Runs Regularly

After every session, inspect the Ended Runs log to record your session duration, win rate, and net profit into your trading journal.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does the Live Run Monitor refresh automatically?

Yes. The monitor updates every two seconds automatically using live WebSocket telemetry from Deriv. You never need to click refresh.

What happens if I accidentally close my browser tab while a bot is running?

Server-side active bot runs continue executing according to your pre-set Stop-Loss and Take-Profit rules. When you open FXnod again, navigate to /autohub and tap "Running Now" to return directly to your live monitor!

Can I change my stop-loss while a bot is actively running?

To protect your trading discipline and prevent emotional tampering mid-streak, active parameters are locked during a run. If you want to change parameters, use the Emergency Force Stop to end the run, adjust your plan, and start fresh.

Is the Emergency Force Stop instant?

Yes. Tapping the button halts the bot execution loop in under 50 milliseconds, ensuring no new contracts are opened.

Where can I see past completed bot sessions?

All past runs are permanently stored at the bottom of the Auto Hub screen under the "Ended Runs" section, complete with historical P&L, timestamps, and contract counts.
