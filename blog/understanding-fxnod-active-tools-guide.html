Understanding FXnod Active Tools: How to Unlock and Manage Premium Trading Tools

The FXnod Active Tools discovery ecosystem: centralized licensing, cloud-synced bot updates, and transparent modular management.

When you step into a professional workshop, you don't just find a single hammer—you find an organized toolbox filled with specialized instruments, each built for a specific job.

FXnod is built on the exact same philosophy.

Rather than locking traders into a rigid, one-size-fits-all software package, FXnod is designed as a modular ecosystem powered by Active Tools. From high-frequency digit engines and directional trend scanners to specialized risk management suites, the Active Tools hub is your centralized command center for discovering, unlocking, and managing your trading capabilities.

Instead of wondering what features your account has access to, the Active Tools hub gives you 100% transparent visibility over every tool's status, expiration timeline, and capabilities.

Understanding Tool Status Badges

Every tool in your catalog displays a clear status badge so you always know where you stand:

Active (Unlocked)

The tool is fully unlocked, verified, and ready to deploy on both Web and Mobile. If it is on a monthly or quarterly plan, your remaining countdown days are clearly displayed.

Inactive (Available)

The tool is available in the catalog but has not been unlocked on your profile yet. Tapping on it lets you inspect its capabilities, watch tutorial clips, and review pricing options.

Expired (Preserved)

A previously active subscription has ended. Your settings and historical trade logs are safely preserved, and you can reactivate the tool with a single click whenever you wish.

Cloud-Synced Updates: Always Up-to-Date

One of the greatest features of FXnod’s modern cloud architecture is Dynamic Cloud Synchronization:

On older trading platforms, whenever developers release a new bot or update a strategy, users are forced to download zip files, edit code files, reconfigure settings, or manually re-upload XML scripts.

On FXnod:

  • When the engineering team rolls out a new algorithm or adds fresh presets to the Auto Hub, they sync directly to your Active Tools screen in the cloud.
  • You get instant access to the latest optimizations across both your web browser and mobile app without ever needing manual file downloads, updates, or reinstalls!

How to Manage & Renew Expired Tools

Managing your active subscriptions on FXnod is transparent and stress-free:

  1. Proactive Expiration Reminders: When a tool approaches its renewal date, the card badge highlights the remaining days so you are never caught unprepared.
  2. Zero Auto-Charges: FXnod never charges hidden fees or unexpected recurring credit card debits. You only renew when you consciously choose to click renew.
  3. 1-Click Instant Renewal: If a tool expires, simply tap on the card, select your preferred duration, and tap Pay with Wallet Balance to instantly resume your access.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Do unlocked tools work on both Web and Mobile?

Yes, 100%! Your tool licenses are tied directly to your FXnod user profile. Unlocking a tool on your desktop browser automatically unlocks it on your mobile phone via the FXnod Mobile App, and vice versa.

Where do I fund my balance to unlock tools?

You fund your internal FXnod Wallet via crypto deposits or internal balance transfers. This wallet is completely separate from your Deriv trading balance.

Will I lose my bot settings if a tool subscription expires?

No. Your saved preferences, custom presets, and historical trade logs remain permanently preserved in your cloud profile. The moment you renew the tool, everything is right where you left it.

Are new bots added to existing tools for free?

Yes! When updates or new bots are rolled out to an active tool (such as the Auto Hub), all active license holders receive the updates automatically via cloud-sync at no extra cost.

Can I upgrade from a 1-Month plan to a Lifetime plan later?

Yes. You can upgrade your subscription tier at any time directly through the Active Tools bottom sheet by choosing a longer duration or Lifetime tier.
