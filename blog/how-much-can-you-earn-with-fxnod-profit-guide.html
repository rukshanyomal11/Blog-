3 Fatal Traps That Destroy Profitability

If you want to stay in the profitable minority of traders, avoid these three deadly habits:

🚨 1. "Just One More Run"

Your bot hits its $3 target in five minutes. Instead of closing the app, greed whispers: "That was fast, let's run it again!" Continuous running inevitably walks you into statistical chop.

⚠️ 2. Under-Capitalizing

Running a $20 Stop-Loss plan with only $20 in your account gives zero cushion against variance. Always maintain at least a 3x buffer ($60+ for a $20 plan). Review: How to Read the Loss Progression Sheet.

🛑 3. Revenge Trading

If a tough streak hits your Stop-Loss, accept it calmly. The stop-loss successfully protected 70% of your account. Do not manually increase stakes to "win it back immediately."
