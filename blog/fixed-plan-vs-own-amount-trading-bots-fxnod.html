How Own Amount Bots Work

Own Amount bots give you the driver’s seat. If you have a unique account size (for example, exactly $12.50 or $500) or prefer a specific money management philosophy, you can customize every single parameter:

  1. Custom Starting Stake: Enter any valid stake (e.g., $0.50 for micro-testing, or $5.00 for larger accounts).
  2. Custom Stop Loss & Take Profit: Set exact dollar thresholds tailored to your personal daily goals.
  3. Choice of Staking Modes:
    • Same Stake (Flat): Every trade uses the identical dollar amount. The safest way to trade, completely eliminating streak compounding.
    • Gentle Step: Adds one starting stake on a loss and removes one on a win. Recovers drawdowns gradually without exponential stake growth.
    • Martingale: Multiplies the stake after a loss to recover the streak in a single win, strictly bounded by your Stop-Loss ceiling.
    • Reverse Martingale: Multiplies stakes during winning streaks and resets after a loss to capitalize on hot runs.

The Big Advantage of Own Amount Bots

Total Flexibility. You can fine-tune your parameters to match your exact trading style, whether you prefer ultra-conservative flat stakes or customized recovery steps.

A Simple Decision Guide: Which Should You Choose?

Not sure which model to pick today? Use this quick checklist:

Choose a Fixed Plan Bot if:

  • ✅ You are new to automated Deriv trading.
  • ✅ You have an account balance between $30 and $200.
  • ✅ You want a battle-tested setup without worrying about math.
  • ✅ You want the speed of 1-click execution on mobile or web.

Choose an Own Amount Bot if:

  • ✅ You want to trade with micro-stakes (like $0.50) on a very small account.
  • ✅ You have an account balance larger than $500 and want custom limits.
  • ✅ You prefer low-risk staking methods like Same Stake (Flat) or Gentle Step.
  • ✅ You have a specific daily target you calculated for your trading journal.
