Why Connecting via API Token Is 100% Safe

Before we begin, let’s clear up what an "API Token" actually is in plain, everyday language.

The Valet Key Analogy: Controlled Access Without Risk

Think of an API Token as a valet key for your car. When you give a valet parking attendant your valet key, they can drive your car into a designated parking space, but they cannot open your locked glove compartment, access your trunk, or drive away with your car permanently.

Similarly, when you create an API token for FXnod:

  • ✅ You allow FXnod to view your balance and place bot trades when you tell a strategy to start.
  • ❌ You do NOT give FXnod permission to withdraw, transfer, or deposit your money.
  • ❌ You do NOT share your Deriv account password or personal banking credentials.

Your money stays 100% inside your Deriv account at all times, protected by your personal banking details, Deriv’s multi-factor security, and global regulatory standards. Step-by-Step: How to Link Deriv to FXnod in 60 Seconds

Follow these four clear steps to connect your account:

1

Open Your Deriv API Settings

Log in to your personal trading account on Deriv.com.

  1. In the top-right corner of your screen, click on your Profile icon (or open the main side menu on mobile).
  2. Click on Account Settings.
  3. Scroll down the left navigation menu to the Security & Safety section and click on API Token.
2

Create Your New Connection Token

On the API Token page, you will see a simple form with a name field and a few permission checkboxes:

  1. Token Name: Type a friendly name so you remember what it is used for, such as My FXnod Bot.
  2. Choose Permissions (Very Important):
    • Check the box for Read (allows FXnod to display your balance and trade results).
    • Check the box for Trade (allows your bots to execute contracts).
    • Leave everything else UNCHECKED (do NOT check "Admin" or "Payments").
  3. Click the red Create button.
  4. Your new token string will appear on your screen. Click the small Copy icon next to it.
3

Paste Your Token into FXnod

Now, switch over to your FXnod dashboard (either in your web browser or inside the mobile app):

  1. Log in to your FXnod account.
  2. In the navigation menu, go to Settings and select Deriv Connection (or simply tap the Deriv Status badge located in the top bar).
  3. Click inside the "Enter Deriv API Token" text box.
  4. Paste the token key you copied from Deriv.
  5. Click the "Connect Account" button.
4

Confirm Your Connection

Within two seconds, the screen will automatically refresh:

  • A bright green "Connected" badge will appear at the top.
  • Your official Deriv Account ID (such as CR123456 or VRTC123456) will show up.
  • Your Live Balance will display immediately on the dashboard.

Congratulations! Your FXnod platform is now linked and ready to run bots safely!

To understand why your funds can never be touched or transferred, look at how Deriv's official security boundaries work:

Read ✅ YES Displays live account balance, open positions, and market tick history ❌ Impossible
Trade ✅ YES Places bot contracts (e.g., Rise/Fall, Digits) when you start a bot ❌ Impossible
Payments / Cashier ❌ NEVER Depositing or withdrawing money to banks, cards, or crypto wallets ❌ Blocked by Deriv

Because FXnod operates strictly under Read and Trade permissions, Deriv's central banking server will flatly reject any withdrawal or transfer request sent through that token. Withdrawals can only ever be authorized by you through Deriv's official website using your personal 2FA credentials.

How to Switch Between Virtual (Demo) and Real Accounts

FXnod gives you the flexibility to practice risk-free on your Demo account before deploying real capital.

If you want to connect both your Demo and Real accounts:

  1. On Deriv, click your account balance dropdown and switch your active account to Demo.
  2. Go back to Account Settings > API Token and create a new token named FXnod Demo (with Read and Trade checked).
  3. Paste this token into FXnod.
  4. You can now switch between your Demo and Real balances right from the top navigation bar of your FXnod screen at any time!

How to Disconnect or Revoke Access Anytime

You are always in complete control of your connection. If you ever want to disconnect FXnod from Deriv, you can do it in two easy ways:

  • From Inside FXnod: Go to Settings > Deriv Connection and click the red "Disconnect" button. This removes the token from your active session.
  • From Inside Deriv: Log in to Deriv, navigate to Account Settings > API Token, locate your FXnod token in the list, and click the red Delete (Trash) icon.

The instant you click Delete on Deriv, Deriv immediately revokes the token. Even if FXnod attempted to fetch tick data, Deriv’s servers would instantly deny access.

Common Mistakes to Avoid When Connecting

Connecting is remarkably straightforward, but keeping an eye out for these three minor mistakes will ensure a smooth, error-free setup:

1. Accidental Extra Spaces When Copying

When selecting and copying your token text on Deriv, make sure you don't accidentally highlight an empty space at the very beginning or end. An extra space will cause FXnod to show an "Invalid Token Format" message. Always use the built-in copy icon on Deriv when possible.

2. Selecting "Payments" or "Admin" Permissions

FXnod does not need or want payments access. Stick strictly to Read and Trade. Keeping other boxes unchecked maintains your strict security posture.

3. Changing Your Deriv Password

If you reset or update your Deriv account password, Deriv automatically deactivates all active API tokens for security reasons. If this occurs, your FXnod status will simply switch to "Disconnected." Just create a fresh token on Deriv and paste it into FXnod.

Can FXnod take money from my Deriv account?

No, never. The token permissions you select only allow viewing account data and opening trade contracts. Deriv's system completely blocks any withdrawal or transfer attempts through an API token.

Does it cost anything to connect my Deriv account to FXnod?

No. Connecting your Deriv account to FXnod is completely free. You can test all 22 Auto Hub bots on your demo account without paying anything.

Can I connect using my smartphone?

Yes! You can complete the exact same steps on your mobile phone browser or inside the FXnod Mobile App.

What should I do if my connection shows "Disconnected"?

This usually means your token has expired, your internet connection briefly paused, or you changed your Deriv password. Simply open Settings > Deriv Connection, enter a fresh token from Deriv, and click Connect.

Can I connect more than one Deriv account?

Yes. You can manage multiple linked accounts (such as a Real account and a Demo account) under one FXnod profile.
