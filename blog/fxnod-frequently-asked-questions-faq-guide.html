Q: Can FXnod ever withdraw or touch my money from Deriv?

No. It is technically and mathematically impossible.

When you connect your Deriv account to FXnod using an API token, you grant only two restricted permissions: Read (to display your balance) and Trade (to open bot contracts on your command). You never grant "Cashier" or "Payments" access. All deposits and withdrawals take place exclusively through Deriv’s official website, protected by your personal banking credentials and 2FA. Risk Management & Capital Protection

Q: How does the "Refused Stake" safeguard work?

The Refused Stake rule is FXnod’s ultimate protection against account wipeouts. Before placing any recovery trade, the engine calculates whether the next stake would cause your total session loss to exceed your pre-set Stop-Loss ceiling. If it would, the engine automatically refuses to place the order and halts the bot safely.

Q: What is the Shadow Lane (Phantom Trading)?

The Shadow Lane is an automated scouting feature. When enabled on supported bots, the bot tests live market ticks in virtual "phantom" mode with $0 real money at risk. It only switches to live execution after an unfavorable streak passes, dramatically reducing your drawdown risk. Read our detailed guide in FXnod Shadow Lane: Phantom Mode Guide.

Q: What is the Loss Progression Sheet?

Available on every bot setup card by tapping "View Loss Run →", this interactive table shows you the exact trade-by-trade stake ladder, cumulative drawdown, and number of consecutive losses your selected plan can absorb before you trade. See How to Read the FXnod Loss Progression Sheet.

Q: How can I avoid blowing my trading account?

We recommend following the "3x Buffer Rule" (maintaining a balance at least 3x your plan's stop loss) and avoiding the top traps explained in Top 5 Mistakes Deriv Bot Traders Make (And How to Avoid Them).
