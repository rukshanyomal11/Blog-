Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Do I have to re-download the mobile app every time a new bot is added?

No! New bots, strategy rules, and Auto Hub updates sync directly through the cloud. You will only occasionally update the app through standard app stores for major operating system compatibility improvements.

Will an update reset my saved bot settings or preferences?

No. Your personal profile, custom presets, API connections, and trade histories are stored safely and independently. Cloud updates simply add new features without touching your saved data.

How often are new updates rolled out?

The FXnod development team pushes cloud optimizations and engine refinements continuously on a weekly basis, with major bot expansions arriving regularly.

Can I request a specific bot or strategy to be added?

Yes! FXnod actively gathers feedback through its official Telegram and community support portals. Popular, mathematically sound strategy requests are regularly evaluated for inclusion in the Auto Hub.

What happens if an update occurs while my bot is actively running?

Active bot sessions execute within their own protected runtime thread according to their pre-set rules. An update to the catalog will never interrupt or alter a bot currently in the middle of a trade.
