While digit contracts like Even/Odd or Differs focus purely on the last decimal digit of a price, many traders prefer traditional market movement: is the price going up, or is it going down?

On Deriv, Rise / Fall contracts represent the purest form of directional trading. You simply predict whether the market price will finish higher (Rise) or lower (Fall) than your entry point after a set number of ticks.

Combined with Deriv’s legendary Volatility Indices—which simulate real-world financial market movements 24/7 without being affected by economic news or bank holidays—Rise / Fall trading offers consistent, round-the-clock action with balanced payouts of around 95% per win.

How Rise / Fall Contracts Work (The Directional Basics)

Unlike traditional forex trading, where you have to calculate pip values, spreads, and leverage, a Rise / Fall contract on Deriv is refreshingly simple:

┌──────────────────────────────────────┐ ┌──────────────────────────────────────┐ │ RISE CONTRACT │ │ FALL CONTRACT │ ├──────────────────────────────────────┤ ├──────────────────────────────────────┤ │ • You predict price will finish │ │ • You predict price will finish │ │ HIGHER than your entry tick │ │ LOWER than your entry tick │ │ • Win condition: Exit Price > Entry │ │ • Win condition: Exit Price < Entry │ │ • Payout: ~95% profit on win │ │ • Payout: ~95% profit on win │ └──────────────────────────────────────┘ └──────────────────────────────────────┘

You can choose your contract execution speed:

  • 1-Tick Speed: Super-fast execution where the outcome settles on the very next second. Ideal for rapid micro-scalping.
  • 5-Tick Momentum: Gives the market five ticks to develop a mini-trend wave before contract settlement. (Compare this with digit parity in our guide: Even / Odd Trading Bots Explained).

Because Rise and Fall have an equal natural probability, a winning $1.00 trade returns roughly $1.95 ($0.95 in clean profit).

What is the win rate of a Rise / Fall bot?

Because directional price movement has an equal 50/50 probability, win rates typically hover around 50% to 55%. With disciplined recovery ladders and automated profit-taking, bots can generate consistent net profit over time.

What is the payout for Rise / Fall contracts on Deriv?
