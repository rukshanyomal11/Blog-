The 4 Bot Families Explained (In Plain English)

The 22 bots in the Auto Hub are divided into four specialized families, each engineered to target a distinct synthetic market behavior:

[ 1. Matches / Differs ] ──► Digit prediction & high-frequency probability [ 2. Even / Odd ] ──► Digit parity & alternating tick streaks [ 3. Over / Under ] ──► Barrier digit thresholds with probability skew [ 4. Rise / Fall ] ──► Directional momentum on Volatility Indices

Matches / Differs

High Win-Rate

How It Works: The bot inspects the very last digit of each price tick (0 through 9).

The Strategy: Differs strategies predict that the next tick's last digit will NOT match a target number. Because there are 9 winning numbers and only 1 losing number out of 10, these bots achieve winning rates above 90% per trade.

Best For: High-frequency, steady small gains and disciplined target runs.

Even / Odd

Parity Balance

How It Works: Every price tick finishes in either an Even digit (0, 2, 4, 6, 8) or an Odd digit (1, 3, 5, 7, 9).

The Strategy: The bot tracks recent parity sequences to detect runs and alternating distribution patterns, triggering 1-tick orders based on mathematical parity rules.

Best For: Fast-paced sessions and balanced risk-to-reward ratios.

Over / Under

Barrier Skew

How It Works: Trades evaluate whether the final tick lands above (Over) or below (Under) a specified threshold number.

The Strategy: An Under 7 bot wins whenever the tick ends in 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, or 6. This establishes a strong statistical edge on individual contract outcomes.

Best For: Conservative traders looking for wide safety margins.

Rise / Fall

Trend Waves

How It Works: Evaluates overall directional price action—predicting whether the market will finish higher (Rise) or lower (Fall) after a set tick count.

The Strategy: Specifically calibrated for Deriv's most liquid Volatility Indices (from Volatility 10 up to 100), automatically filtering unsupported markets so you never place an invalid trade.

Best For: Traders who love riding short-term directional momentum.
