Let’s explore each zone in detail.

1. The Header & Status Bar: Real-Time Account Transparency

Located at the very top of both the Web dashboard and Mobile app, the Status Bar provides immediate, at-a-glance visibility into your trading environment:

Connection Status Badge

🟢 Connected: Live encrypted WebSocket with Deriv.
🟡 Connecting: Renewing token handshake.
🔴 Needs Reconnect: Token expired or disconnected.

Account Mode Badge

Virtual USD (Demo): Highlighted in vibrant purple to guarantee 100% risk-free testing.
Real USD: Displayed when live capital is linked.

Live Balance Display

Continuously synchronizes with your official Deriv wallet balance after every single contract settlement, without needing page reloads.

100% Non-Custodial Security Architecture

FXnod connects directly to Deriv via official API tokens scoped strictly to Read and Trade. FXnod has zero authority to withdraw, deposit, or transfer your funds. Your money never leaves Deriv’s secure custody.

2. The Auto Hub: Your 22-Bot Command Center

The Auto Hub is the heart of FXnod. Accessible via the main navigation menu, it organizes 22 pre-configured algorithmic bots into four streamlined category tabs:

  • Matches / Differs: High-frequency digit variance bots engineered for mathematical edge and rapid execution.
  • Even / Odd: Digit parity strategies designed for ultra-fast 1-tick entries on volatile synthetics.
  • Over / Under: Probability-skewed strategies with conservative entry filters.
  • Rise / Fall: Directional momentum bots filtered strictly to supported Volatility Indices (1HZ10V to R_100).

Every bot card in the catalog highlights its execution pace, strategy tier (Fixed Plan vs. Custom Amount), and a "Set Up" action button to inspect parameters before deploying.

3. Bot Setup Screen: Complete Transparency Before You Launch

When you select any bot in the Auto Hub, you are taken to its Setup Screen. FXnod ensures you never enter a trade blindly:

  1. Bilingual "How It Works" Guide: Clear, step-by-step breakdown of entry pace, winning conditions, and actual tick examples in plain English.
  2. Plan Choice Cards ($20, $50, $100 & Custom): Pre-engineered risk ladders calibrated with optimal stake multipliers and stop-loss boundaries. If a plan includes contract take-profit (contractTakeProfit), it clearly displays: "Position closes at profit of $X."
  3. The "View Loss Run" (Loss Progression Sheet): Clicking this button opens an interactive modal table showing exact stakes per consecutive loss, cumulative drawdown, maximum losing streaks absorbed, and the Refused Stake threshold.

4. The Live Run Monitor: Supervise Every Single Tick

Once you launch a bot, FXnod transitions into the Live Run Monitor (/autohub/runs/:id). This screen gives you complete operational oversight:

  • Key Metrics Grid: Displays Net Profit/Loss, Total Runs, Wins, Losses, and current Win Rate.
  • Stop-Loss & Take-Profit Progress Meters: Color-coded dynamic progress bars showing exactly how close you are to your profit target or safety stop.
  • Shadow Lane Gauge: If the bot utilizes phantom trades, this gauge visualizes background simulated runs before real funds are committed.
  • Real-Time Entries Log: An auditable stream of recent trades detailing contract IDs, buy prices, payout amounts, and settlement status.
  • Emergency Force Stop: A prominent red button that immediately terminates the bot thread in milliseconds if you choose to exit early.

5. Active Tools & Subscription Hub

The Active Tools section (/discover/tools) displays your unlocked software suite:

  • Tool Status Badges: Shows whether an active tool is Active, Inactive, or Expired.
  • Plan Period & Expiry: Tracks your subscription timeline (1 Month, 3 Months, 6 Months, or Lifetime).
  • Plan Upgrade Sheet: Allows you to unlock premium tiers, apply promotional coupon codes, lock quotes with a 15-minute countdown, and pay directly from your internal wallet balance.
  • Dynamic Cloud Sync: When new bots or strategy engines are released, they sync automatically to your dashboard without requiring manual app updates.

6. Settings & Security Management

Under the Settings panel, you have granular control over your profile and device security:

  • Deriv Connection Manager: Easily switch between different Deriv accounts, update API tokens, or re-authorize with 1-click OAuth.
  • Biometric App Lock (Mobile): Enable Face ID, Fingerprint, or 4-digit PIN authentication to guard your active mobile trading sessions.
  • Currency & Display Preferences: Choose light or dark themes and customize how currency values are formatted across the dashboard.

Pro Tips for Getting the Most Out of Your Dashboard

  1. Verify Your Account Badge First: Make it a habit to check the top status bar to ensure you are on Virtual USD (Demo) whenever testing unfamiliar bot presets.
  2. Review the Loss Run Before Every Session: Never launch a bot without clicking "View Loss Run →" to confirm that your account balance comfortably exceeds the plan's stop-loss cushion.
  3. Use the Emergency Stop When News Hits: If high-impact economic news creates unpredictable market spikes, use the red Force Stop button to pause your session safely.
  4. Monitor Ended Runs for Insights: Check the Ended Runs list at the bottom of the Auto Hub to analyze past session durations, profit curves, and win rates.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What does "Needs Reconnect" mean in the dashboard?

This alert appears when your Deriv API token has expired or requires renewal. Simply navigate to Settings, generate a fresh token on Deriv, and paste it to restore your live connection in seconds.

Can I run multiple bots at the same time on the dashboard?

To ensure responsible risk management and prevent overlapping margin calls, FXnod is configured to prioritize disciplined, focused bot runs. Always monitor your active session before initiating additional trading threads.

Does the dashboard update in real time without refreshing the page?

Yes. FXnod uses high-speed WebSocket telemetry. Every tick, trade entry, contract outcome, and balance change reflects instantly on your screen without manual browser refreshes.

Where can I see my historical trading receipts?

Completed sessions are logged in the Ended Runs section of the Auto Hub, while subscription receipts and wallet transactions are stored permanently under the Wallet & Orders tab.

Is the FXnod Dashboard free to explore?

Yes! Anyone can create an account, explore the complete dashboard, review the bot catalog, and run simulated bots on a Deriv Virtual Demo account at zero cost.
