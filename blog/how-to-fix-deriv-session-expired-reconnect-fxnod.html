First, Take a Deep Breath: Everything Is 100% Safe

Have you ever logged into FXnod to start a trading bot, only to see a notification saying "Drive Reconnect Required" or "Deriv Session Expired"—even though you already connected your account in the past?

If this has happened to you, take a deep breath: nothing is broken, your account is not banned, and your money is 100% safe.

Standard Regulatory Security Protection

This notification is actually a standard security feature built by Deriv to protect your account. Just like your mobile banking app logs you out after a period of inactivity to prevent unauthorized access, Deriv periodically refreshes security keys to ensure that only you can authorize trade commands.

In this quick guide, you will learn why this notification appears, how to resolve it in under 30 seconds, and simple tips to keep your connection uninterrupted during your trading sessions.

The 30-Second Fix: How to Reconnect Step-by-Step

Resolving the reconnect notification takes less than half a minute. Follow these four quick steps:

1

Open the Connection Screen

Inside the FXnod dashboard or mobile app, simply tap on the "Drive Reconnect" banner or navigate to Settings > Deriv Connection. For an overview of the interface, see our FXnod Dashboard Complete Walkthrough.

2

Grab a Fresh Token from Deriv

In another tab or on your phone browser, open Deriv.com and log in:

  1. Go to your Profile > Account Settings > API Token.
  2. Under Create New Token:
    • Give it a name like FXnod Daily Token.
    • Check only Read and Trade.
  3. Click Create and tap Copy next to the new token string.

If you need a refresher on token creation, check our guide: How to Connect Deriv to FXnod Safely.

3

Paste into FXnod Connection Box

Switch back to your FXnod tab or mobile app:

  1. Paste the copied key into the Enter Deriv API Token box.
  2. Tap Connect Account.
4

Confirm Your Connection Restored

Within two seconds, the reconnect alert will vanish:

  • The status bar turns bright green with the "Connected" badge.
  • Your live balance reloads automatically.
  • Your Auto Hub bots are immediately ready to launch!

Are Your Saved Bot Settings Safe During a Reconnect?

Yes, 100%!

A temporary Deriv session reconnect does not erase your preferences, selected plans, or saved bot configurations. Your FXnod profile stores your favorite presets separately from the broker connection. Once you paste the fresh token, all your settings reappear exactly where you left them.

Furthermore, if a bot had already completed a run, your trade history and session receipts remain permanently logged under your Ended Runs section.

4 Simple Tips to Prevent Frequent Disconnections

If you want to keep your trading sessions running smoothly with minimal reconnect interruptions, follow these best practices:

1. Use a Dedicated Token for FXnod

Do not use the same API token across multiple different apps or scripts. Create one unique token specifically labeled FXnod-App.

2. Stick to "Read" and "Trade" Only

Selecting extra unnecessary permissions (like Admin) can cause Deriv's automated security filters to flag and expire tokens more frequently.

3. Avoid Constant Network Hopping

If running an active bot on your phone, try to stay on a stable Wi-Fi or steady mobile network rather than switching back and forth mid-session. See our FXnod Mobile App Guide for optimal network settings.

4. Keep Your Deriv Tab Logged In

If you trade on a laptop, keeping your official Deriv tab open in the background helps maintain active session handshakes smoothly.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can a session disconnect cause me to lose money?

No. If an active trade contract was already dispatched to Deriv, that single contract settles independently on Deriv’s servers according to its tick duration. If a session disconnects, FXnod’s safety guard stops the bot from opening new trades until you reconnect, protecting your balance from unattended market swings.

Why did I get a reconnect alert right after creating a token?

This usually happens if an accidental extra whitespace character was copied along with the token key, or if the token was created on a Demo account while trying to connect in Real mode. Read our guide on How to Test FXnod Bots on Demo to ensure your token matches your intended mode.

How often do Deriv API tokens expire?

Depending on your network stability and Deriv’s routine security sweeps, tokens can remain active for weeks or may require renewal every few days. Refreshing your token takes only 30 seconds.

Do I have to pay anything to reconnect?

No. Reconnecting your Deriv account to FXnod is completely free and can be done as many times as needed.

Will reinstalling the FXnod app fix the reconnect alert?

You do not need to reinstall the app. A reconnect alert is purely an authorization handshake between Deriv and your platform. Simply pasting a fresh token resolves it immediately.

By taking 30 seconds to paste a fresh token, you restore full access and ensure that every trade is backed by an encrypted, verified connection.
