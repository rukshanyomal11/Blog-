Step 3: Link Your Deriv Account Safely (API Token Setup)

To execute trades on Deriv markets, FXnod needs an encrypted bridge. This is achieved through Deriv's official API token architecture.

How to Generate Your Deriv API Token Safely:

  1. Log in to your personal Deriv.com account.
  2. Click your profile avatar in the top-right corner and go to Account Settings > API Token.
  3. Under Create New Token, enter a recognizable name (e.g., FXnod-Bot-Link).
  4. Select Only These Two Permissions:
    ✅ Read: Allows FXnod to fetch live balances and contract outcomes.
    ✅ Trade: Allows FXnod to place bot contracts on your command.
    ❌ DO NOT Select "Admin" or "Payments": FXnod never requests or needs cashier access.
  5. Click Create and copy your newly generated token.

Connecting the Token in FXnod:

  1. In FXnod, navigate to Settings > Deriv Connection (or click the Deriv Status badge in the top bar).
  2. Paste your copied API token into the input field.
  3. Click Connect Account.
  4. Within two seconds, FXnod will establish an encrypted handshake. You will see a green Connected badge alongside your Deriv Account ID and balance!

Step 4: Configure Crucial Account Security Settings

FXnod takes user security seriously. Before launching your first bot, take one minute to turn on these built-in safeguards:

1. App Lock & Biometrics (Mobile App)

If you are using the FXnod Mobile App, navigate to Settings > Security and enable App Lock. You can protect your app using Face ID, Fingerprint, or a 4-digit PIN to prevent anyone else from touching your active runs.

2. Auto-Disconnect Protection

Under your connection settings, verify that Session Auto-Refresh is enabled. If network latency or a temporary server drop occurs, FXnod's background guard automatically pauses active orders rather than firing blind trades.

Common Setup Mistakes & How to Avoid Them

Even though FXnod setup is straightforward, keep these points in mind:

  • Mistake 1: Giving full Admin permissions on Deriv. Always stick to Read and Trade only. Never grant cashier rights to any third-party interface.
  • Mistake 2: Typing the token manually. Always use the copy-paste button. An accidental whitespace character can trigger an "Invalid Token" notification.
  • Mistake 3: Starting directly on a Real account. Always run at least 20–30 simulated trades on your Virtual account to familiarize yourself with tick speed and drawdown mechanics. Read our complete guide on Managing Risk When Using a Trading Bot >

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is it free to create an FXnod account?

Yes. Creating an FXnod account and linking it to a Deriv Virtual Demo account is 100% free with no credit card required.

Can FXnod access or withdraw my trading funds?

Absolutely not. Deriv’s API token infrastructure strictly prohibits withdrawals through third-party tokens. FXnod only holds execution permissions. All deposits and withdrawals take place exclusively on Deriv's official cashier portal.

Can I use FXnod on both my phone and laptop at the same time?

Yes! FXnod provides real-time cloud synchronization. You can configure a bot on your PC and monitor or stop the live run from your mobile phone on the go.

What should I do if my Deriv connection says "Disconnected"?

Deriv tokens occasionally expire if you change your Deriv password or reset security keys. Simply go to Settings > Deriv Connection, generate a fresh token on Deriv, and paste it to reconnect in seconds.

How old do I need to be to use FXnod?

Because FXnod connects directly to Deriv, you must meet the legal age requirements to hold a Deriv account in your jurisdiction (typically 18 years or older).
