The moment you launch a bot, the app transitions into the Live Run Monitor:

Never guess your risk. Before starting any bot run, tap "View Loss Run" to open an interactive modal bottom sheet. It breaks down every potential Martingale step:

  • Confirmation of the Refused Stake safeguard (the engine stops trading automatically before exceeding your Stop Loss).

Keep your active trading safe from curious eyes or accidental taps. The app features native biometric integration:

Best Practices for Mobile Bot Trading

To keep your mobile trading smooth, safe, and consistent, adhere to these professional operational guidelines:

  1. Keep Mobile Data Stable: Ensure you have a reliable 4G/5G or Wi-Fi connection. While server-side trades process independently on cloud engines, real-time live monitoring relies on uninterrupted WebSocket telemetry.
  2. Never Disable App Lock: Always keep your fingerprint or PIN lock turned on so active sessions cannot be accidentally disrupted or touched inside your pocket.
  3. Always Check the Account Badge: Look at the top badge before starting any bot to confirm whether you are on Virtual USD (Demo) or Real USD.
  4. Use Contract Take-Profit: Enable contract-level profit targets (contractTakeProfit) to exit positions early and bank profits automatically.
  5. Set Realistic Daily Targets: Let the bot achieve its target (e.g., $1 to $5 daily on smaller accounts), then close the app. Consistency beats overtrading every time.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does the FXnod Mobile App consume heavy battery or data?

No. The app uses lightweight WebSocket streams and optimized Flutter rendering. It uses minimal data and battery compared to graphic-heavy charting platforms.

Will my bot stop running if I close the app or receive a phone call?

Server-side active bot runs continue to execute their current lifecycle on the cloud engine according to your pre-set Stop-Loss and Take-Profit rules. When you open the app again, it re-syncs instantly with the live run status.

Is the mobile app safe if my phone is lost or stolen?

Yes. Thanks to the built-in Biometric App Lock, no one can open the app without your face, fingerprint, or PIN. Furthermore, because FXnod cannot withdraw funds from Deriv, your money remains completely secure in your Deriv wallet.

Can I deposit or withdraw money through the mobile app?

All deposit and withdrawal transactions are handled directly through Deriv’s official cashier portal for maximum security. FXnod never touches your banking or payment credentials.

Is the mobile app free to use?

Yes! You can download the FXnod Mobile App and run all 22 Auto Hub bots on your Deriv Demo/Virtual account completely free of charge.
