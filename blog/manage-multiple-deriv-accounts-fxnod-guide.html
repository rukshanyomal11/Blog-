Step-by-Step: How to Link and Switch Accounts in FXnod

Linking multiple accounts takes less than two minutes. Follow these simple steps:

1

Generate Separate Tokens on Deriv

Log in to your Deriv account and generate an API token for each account you want to connect:

  1. For Demo: Select your Virtual USD account in Deriv. Go to Account Settings > API Token, create a token named FXnod Demo with Read and Trade checked, and copy it.
  2. For Real: Switch your Deriv account to Real USD. Repeat the process to create a token named FXnod Real with Read and Trade checked, and copy it.

Need step-by-step token instructions? See our guide: How to Connect Deriv to FXnod Safely.

2

Save Both Accounts in FXnod Settings

Open your FXnod dashboard (Web or Mobile) and link the tokens:

  1. Navigate to Settings > Deriv Connection.
  2. Paste your Demo Token first and click Connect. FXnod saves your Virtual account details.
  3. Tap Add Another Account and paste your Real Token.
  4. Give each account a clear nickname (e.g., "My Demo Tester" and "My Real Account").
3

Switch Accounts in 1 Click

Once your accounts are linked, you never need to visit the settings page again to switch:

  1. Look at the top status bar of your FXnod screen.
  2. Click or tap directly on the Account Badge.
  3. A clean dropdown list appears displaying your linked accounts with their live balances.
  4. Tap the account you wish to activate.
  5. In less than two seconds, FXnod switches the active connection, updates your balance display, and readies your bots!
4

Verify Badge Color & Launch

Confirm the top status badge color (Blue for Demo, Green for Real) and launch your chosen bot from the Auto Hub with total confidence.

What Happens to Active Bots When You Switch Accounts?

A common question is: Can I have a bot running on Demo while I check my Real balance?

To keep your trading risk completely contained and disciplined, FXnod binds each active bot session to the specific account on which it was launched:

  • An active bot on Demo will only trade on that virtual balance.
  • It is technically impossible for a demo bot to "spill over" and place orders on your real account.
  • If you switch the active screen, your running session continues its pre-programmed rules independently according to its pre-set Stop-Loss and Take-Profit limits.

Complete Account Isolation

Deriv API tokens are tied to specific Account IDs (e.g. VRTC... vs. CR...). Deriv's broker server flatly rejects any command intended for one account that is dispatched through another token.

Best Practices for Multi-Account Trading

To get the most out of your multi-account setup without confusion, follow these four golden rules:

1. Double-Check the Badge Color

Form the habit of glancing at the top badge color (Blue for Demo, Green for Real) before launching any bot run.

2. Use Clear Nicknames

When saving tokens, give them descriptive names like Demo Sandbox and Real $100 Bankroll for instant recognition.

3. Never Test New Bots on Real First

Run at least 30 to 50 simulated cycles on your Demo account before toggling over to your Real account.

4. Keep Real Account Tokens Fresh

If you change your Deriv password, both your Demo and Real tokens will refresh. Update both in FXnod to prevent session disconnects.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I link more than two Deriv accounts to FXnod?

Yes. You can link multiple Deriv accounts (such as different currency accounts or multiple sub-accounts) under one FXnod profile.

Can an accidental click on Demo place a trade on my Real money?

No. Because Demo and Real accounts use entirely separate security tokens issued by Deriv, a trade dispatched to a Demo token cannot interact with real capital. Deriv's server infrastructure strictly separates demo balances from real funds. Read our full security breakdown: Is FXnod Safe and Legitimate?.

Do I need to pay twice for FXnod tools to use them on multiple accounts?

No! Your FXnod tool subscriptions and Auto Hub bot access belong to your FXnod user profile, not your individual Deriv accounts. Once unlocked, you can use your tools across all your linked Deriv accounts.

Can I disconnect one account without affecting the other?

Yes. In Settings > Deriv Connection, each linked account has its own "Disconnect / Remove" option. You can remove a specific token anytime while keeping your other connections intact.

Does switching accounts on my phone also switch it on my computer?

Yes. FXnod features real-time cloud profile synchronization. When you toggle your active account on your mobile app, your web dashboard reflects the active selection seamlessly.
