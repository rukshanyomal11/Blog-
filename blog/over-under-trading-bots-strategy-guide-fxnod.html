In synthetic trading, finding strategies where the mathematical odds are stacked heavily in your favor is every trader's dream.

While predicting market trends can sometimes feel unpredictable, Over / Under Digit Trading on Deriv offers a refreshing alternative. Instead of trying to guess whether a market will explode upwards or crash downwards over minutes, you simply predict whether the final digit of the next tick will land above or below a chosen barrier number.

By choosing conservative barriers—such as Under 8 or Over 1—you can build setups that win up to 80% of the time.

However, trading high-probability setups manually without a structured recovery plan can be dangerous if a rare losing cluster catches you unprepared.

How Over / Under Digit Trading Works (The Simple Math)

Every single second on Deriv Volatility Indices, the price ticks. The Over / Under contract evaluates only the very last digit of that tick, which is always a number between 0 and 9:

┌──────────────────────────────────────┐ ┌──────────────────────────────────────┐ │ "UNDER 8" SETUP │ │ "OVER 1" SETUP │ ├──────────────────────────────────────┤ ├──────────────────────────────────────┤ │ • Winning Digits: 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7│ │ • Winning Digits: 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9│ │ • Total Winning Outcomes: 8 out of 10│ │ • Total Winning Outcomes: 8 out of 10│ │ • Win Probability: 80% │ │ • Win Probability: 80% │ │ • Losing Digits: 8 and 9 │ │ • Losing Digits: 0 and 1 │ └──────────────────────────────────────┘ └──────────────────────────────────────┘

Here is how different barrier contracts distribute probability:

  • Under 7 Contract: Wins whenever the final digit is 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, or 6 (70% win probability).
  • Under 8 Contract: Wins whenever the final digit is 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, or 7 (80% win probability).
  • Over 1 Contract: Wins whenever the final digit is 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, or 9 (80% win probability).

Because 8 out of every 10 digits are winners in an Under 8 or Over 1 trade, winning streaks occur naturally and frequently.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Which is safer: Under 8 or Under 7?

Under 8 offers a higher win probability (80% vs 70%), meaning trades win more frequently. However, Under 7 offers slightly higher payouts per win. Both are pre-calibrated in the Auto Hub for optimal balance.

Can an Over / Under bot lose multiple trades in a row?

While rare on an 80% win-probability setup, consecutive losses are mathematically possible in any financial market. This is why FXnod enforces strict Stop-Loss boundaries and transparent Loss Progression sheets before you trade.

What is the payout on an Over / Under trade?

Depending on the barrier digit, payouts typically range between 20% and 150%. For high-probability setups like Under 8, payouts hover around 20% to 23% per winning contract.

What is the difference between Over / Under and Even / Odd?
