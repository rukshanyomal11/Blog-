Think of the Shadow Lane like a scout sent ahead of an army:

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does the Shadow Lane cost extra money or require special subscriptions?

No. Bots featuring the Shadow Lane inside the FXnod Auto Hub are ready to use out of the box with your standard access pass.

Does the Shadow Lane use real money for its scout trades?

No, absolutely not. Phantom trades are 100% simulated in the background using live Deriv tick data. Zero dollars are deducted from your balance during shadow scouting.

Why didn't my bot place a trade immediately after I clicked Start?

If the bot has Shadow Lane enabled, it is actively scouting ticks in the background! It is waiting for the pre-programmed market conditions to align before risking your money. You can monitor its progress on the Live Shadow Gauge.

Can I use the Shadow Lane on the FXnod Mobile App?
