How Same Stake Works

Same Stake (often called Flat Staking) is the gold standard of professional risk management.

If you set your starting stake to $1.00, the bot places $1.00 on every single trade—whether it just won five trades in a row or lost two trades in a row:

Trade 1: $1.00 → Trade 2: $1.00 → Trade 3: $1.00 → Trade 4: $1.00

Why Traders Love Same Stake:

🛡️ Zero Wipeout Risk

Because your stakes never increase, an unexpected 4-loss streak only costs you $4.00. Your account balance remains completely safe.

🧘 Total Psychological Calm

You can run your bot without racing heartbeats, sweaty palms, or stress over exponential compounding.

📈 Positive Expectancy

When paired with high win-rate bots (such as Differs with a 90%+ win rate or Under 8 with an 80% win rate), flat staking steadily accumulates profits through positive statistics.

Method 2: Gentle Step – Smart Recovery Without the Drama

How Gentle Step Works

What if you want a mechanism to recover losses, but you refuse to let your stakes multiply exponentially? Gentle Step is the answer.

Instead of multiplying your stake by 2x or 3x, Gentle Step walks a gentle, linear staircase:

  • After a Loss: Add exactly one starting stake unit.
  • After a Win: Subtract exactly one starting stake unit.
  • The Floor: The stake never drops below your initial starting baseline.

Real-World Example of a Gentle Step Sequence:

Suppose you start with a $1.00 starting stake:

Trade Step Outcome Stake Placed Rule Executed
Trade #1 Loss ❌ $1.00 Add 1 starting stake ($1.00 + $1.00 = $2.00)
Trade #2 Loss ❌ $2.00 Add 1 starting stake ($2.00 + $1.00 = $3.00)
Trade #3 WIN ✅ $3.00 Win! Remove 1 stake ($3.00 - $1.00 = $2.00)
Trade #4 WIN ✅ $2.00 Win! Remove 1 stake ($2.00 - $1.00 = $1.00)
Trade #5 Active $1.00 Back at the baseline starting stake!

Notice what happened: during a 2-loss streak, your maximum stake was only $3.00! With Martingale, that same sequence would have escalated past $4.70. How to Choose the Right Staking Style for Your Account

Use this quick decision framework to match your bankroll with the optimal staking model:

Choose Same Stake (Flat) if:

  • Your account balance is under $30.
  • You are an absolute beginner testing automated bots.
  • You trade high-probability bots (Differs 90% or Over/Under 80%).
  • Your primary goal is 100% capital preservation.

Choose Gentle Step if:

  • Your account balance is between $30 and $75.
  • You want to recover small drawdowns faster than flat staking.
  • You dislike exponential multipliers that escalate too fast.
  • You prefer a steady, calculated linear recovery ladder.

Choose Martingale if:

  • Your account balance is $100 or higher.
  • You understand your plan's Stop-Loss ceiling.
  • You want single-win recovery in quick 5-minute sessions.
  • You reviewed the Loss Progression Sheet in advance.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I make a profit with Same Stake (Flat Staking)?

Yes! In strategies where the statistical win rate exceeds the break-even threshold (such as Differs bots winning over 91% of the time, or directional bots riding a strong trend), flat staking accumulates steady net profit without ever risking exponential drawdowns.

What happens if I lose several trades in a row on Gentle Step?

On Gentle Step, your stake only increases by one starting unit per loss (e.g., $1, $2, $3, $4, $5). The growth is linear, not exponential. Furthermore, FXnod’s built-in Stop-Loss safeguard will automatically stop the bot if the total loss reaches your safety boundary.

What is the minimum stake for Same Stake on Deriv?

On Deriv synthetic indices, the minimum allowable stake is typically between $0.35 and $0.50 depending on the specific volatility index and currency.

Which staking style is best for a $20 account?

For a $20 account, Same Stake (Flat) at $0.50 or $1.00 is by far the safest choice. It guarantees that a temporary losing streak cannot wipe out your account.
