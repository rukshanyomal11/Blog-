Step-by-Step: How to Activate Your Virtual Demo Account on FXnod

Follow these simple steps to start simulated trading:

1

Switch to Demo on Deriv

Log in to your personal account on Deriv.com.

  1. In the top-right corner, click on your account balance dropdown.
  2. Click on the Demo tab.
  3. Your active balance will switch to Virtual USD (typically initialized at $10,000.00).
2

Create a Demo API Token

Generate a dedicated connection key for your demo account:

  1. Navigate to Account Settings > API Token inside Deriv.
  2. Enter a friendly name such as FXnod Demo Tester.
  3. Check only two permission boxes:
    • ✅ Read (fetches live balance and contract tick telemetry)
    • ✅ Trade (dispatches test contracts to the market)
  4. Click the red Create button and click Copy next to your new token string.
3

Connect the Token in FXnod

Link the token inside your FXnod control center:

  1. Open FXnod in your web browser or open the mobile app.
  2. Go to Settings > Deriv Connection.
  3. Paste your demo token into the input box and click Connect Account.
  4. Look at the top status bar: you will see a prominent "Virtual USD (Demo)" green badge with your $10,000 virtual balance.
4

Launch Your First Risk-Free Run

Open the Auto Hub, select a beginner-friendly bot (such as an Even / Odd or Differs bot), review the Loss Progression table, and click Start. You are now testing live market automation with zero risk!

What to Observe During Your First Demo Run

Once connected to your Demo account, launch a beginner-friendly bot from the Auto Hub (such as an Even / Odd or Differs bot on the $20 Plan).

As the bot runs, pay close attention to these three core indicators on the Live Run Monitor:

1. The P&L Progress Meter

Watch how the circular progress meter fills up as the bot lands winning trades. Notice how it smoothly tracks your distance to your target profit and displays net earnings in real time.

2. The Loss Cushion & Stake Behavior

Observe what happens when a losing trade occurs: Does the bot use a fixed stake or a gentle step multiplier? Does the loss progression match the "View Loss Run" sheet? Notice how it never exceeds your pre-set Stop-Loss ceiling.

3. The Automatic Shutdown

When the bot reaches its target profit (Take-Profit) or safety boundary (Stop-Loss), watch how the engine automatically halts the session and locks in the final result without human hesitation.

The Psychology of Moving from Demo to Real Money

Here is an honest truth every trader must understand: trading with real money feels completely different than trading with demo money.

When you trade with virtual funds, your brain feels completely relaxed. But when real dollars are on the line, feelings of fear and greed can tempt you to interfere with the bot, pause it too early, or revenge trade manually.

To learn why disciplined automated rules outperform emotional manual trading, read our full breakdown: Automated Trading vs. Manual Trading on Deriv: Why Bots Win on Discipline.

Three Rules for a Smooth Live Transition

  1. Start with the Smallest Possible Stake: On your first live session, use the lowest fixed stake available (e.g., $0.50 or $1.00) with a small $20 plan.
  2. Treat Real Money Exactly Like Demo Money: Trust the pre-programmed mathematical rules. Do not stop the bot halfway through a normal streak out of nervousness.
  3. Set Small Daily Targets: Aim for modest wins (e.g., $1 to $3 per day on a small account). Small consistent gains compound rapidly over time without stress.
