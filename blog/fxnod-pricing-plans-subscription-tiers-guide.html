Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes! If you start with a 1-Month or 3-Month plan and decide you want permanent Lifetime Access, you can easily upgrade directly through the Active Tools screen.

Do I need an active subscription to trade on a Demo account?

You can explore the Auto Hub and test all 22 bots on your free Deriv Virtual Demo account at zero cost. Tool subscriptions are required when deploying tools for live-money trading. Review our walkthrough: How to Test FXnod Bots on a Demo Account.

What happens when my subscription period ends?

When your plan expires, your tool status changes to Expired. Your historical trade data, saved presets, and profile settings are safely preserved. You can renew whenever you are ready with one click.

Does a Lifetime License include future bots?

Yes, 100%! All new bots, strategy updates, and features released in future versions sync automatically to Lifetime license holders via cloud-sync at zero additional charge.

How do I pay for my subscription?

You simply fund your internal FXnod Wallet via crypto deposits or internal balance transfers, select your plan, and click "Pay with Wallet Balance."
