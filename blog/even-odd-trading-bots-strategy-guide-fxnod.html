When traders look for a balanced, high-action market on Deriv, Even / Odd Digit Trading is often the top choice.

Unlike strategies that require predicting complex chart indicators, trend channels, or economic news events, Even / Odd trading is straightforward: you predict whether the final digit of the next market tick will be an Even number (0, 2, 4, 6, 8) or an Odd number (1, 3, 5, 7, 9).

With a clean 50/50 probability split and fast 1-tick contract durations, Even / Odd trading offers balanced payouts of around 95% to 96% per win.

However, trading Even / Odd manually by guessing colors or chasing feelings often leads to emotional mistakes—especially when sudden streaks of four or five consecutive odds appear.

The Biggest Pitfall of Manual Even / Odd Trading: The "Streak Trap"

When trading manually, human psychology often falls into the famous Gambler's Fallacy:

"The market just produced four Odd numbers in a row, so the next tick MUST be Even!"

— The classic cognitive trap that causes retail traders to blow their account balance.

In reality, each price tick is mathematically independent. The market can easily produce five, six, or seven Odd numbers in a row. A manual trader who keeps doubling their stake on "Even" because they feel it is due will quickly run out of capital.
