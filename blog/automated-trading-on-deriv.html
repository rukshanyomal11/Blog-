Core Principle for Beginners

For beginners, the most important step is not finding a bot that promises profits. It is understanding how the platform works, learning how to configure a strategy, and testing it before risking real money. In this guide, you will learn how to get started on Deriv, what to check before running a bot, and how automation tools such as FXnod fit into your trading workflow.

What Is Automated Trading on Deriv?

Automated trading uses software to follow instructions defined by a trader. Depending on the tool, it can monitor specified conditions, generate trading signals, or execute trades automatically.

Deriv provides Deriv Bot, a visual trading automation tool that lets users build strategies using configurable blocks rather than writing code. Its supported markets and trade types depend on the platform and available products.

You can explore the official platform here: Deriv Bot — Official Website.

REALISTIC EXPECTATIONS

Remember that automated trading does not guarantee profits. A bot can follow its instructions correctly and still make losing trades.

How to Start Automated Trading on Deriv

Follow these structured steps to understand the process:

1

Understand the Deriv Platform

Before using automation, familiarise yourself with the Deriv platform, the products available to you, and the relevant trading rules. Make sure you understand the difference between the available markets and trade types. Not every tool supports every product, so check compatibility before choosing an automation solution.

Official resource: Deriv Help Centre >
2

Start With a Demo Account

A demo account lets you practise with virtual funds rather than immediately risking your own money. Use it to learn the platform interface, explore the available settings, and understand how trade outcomes are displayed. Demo results do not guarantee that the same strategy will perform similarly with real funds due to execution conditions and psychology.

3

Explore Deriv Bot

Visit the official Deriv Bot page and follow the platform's current instructions to access the tool. Deriv Bot uses a visual interface with configurable blocks where you select a market, configure trade parameters, and define conditions. Start by understanding each setting instead of immediately running a complicated strategy.

4

Configure Your Trading Rules

A trading strategy needs clear instructions. Before running a bot, identify:

  • Entry conditions: What must happen before a trade is opened?
  • Trade parameters: Which market, trade type, duration, or stake settings are being used?
  • Exit conditions: When should a trade or trading cycle end?
  • Risk controls: What limits should stop further trading?
  • Restart rules: Under what conditions, if any, should the bot continue?

Available settings vary by platform and trade type. Do not assume that every bot provides every risk control.

5

Test the Strategy

Run your strategy in a demo environment where supported. Observe how it behaves under different market conditions and review its trade history. Do not judge a strategy based on just a few winning trades. Consider losing trades, transaction costs, drawdowns, and market transitions.

6

Monitor the Bot

Automation does not mean you can ignore the system completely. Check whether the bot is still running, whether the platform connection is working, and whether its trading activity matches your intended rules. Also check whether the tool runs in your browser (closing the browser can pause its operation) or in another supported environment. Refer to the official Deriv Bot page.

Can You Use an External Trading Automation Tool With Deriv?

Some traders explore third-party tools to manage parts of their trading workflow. However, compatibility is essential. Before connecting an external tool to Deriv, verify:

Platform Support: Whether the tool currently supports Deriv.
Market Compatibility: Which markets and trade types it supports.
Authorisation Method: Whether it requires an account connection, API authorisation, or another integration.
Permission Scopes: What permissions it requests and whether those permissions are strictly necessary.
Demo Testing: Whether demo testing is available before risking capital.
Security & Pricing: What fees, limitations, and security requirements apply.

⚠️ Never share your account password or sensitive credentials with an unverified service. If a tool requests API access, understand the permissions before approving the connection.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

!

Starting With Real Money Too Quickly

Learn the platform and thoroughly test your strategy on a demo account before considering real-money trading.

!

Using a Strategy You Do Not Understand

If you cannot explain why the bot enters or exits a trade, you may not be ready to run it.

!

Assuming Automation Means Guaranteed Profit

Bots follow instructions; they cannot eliminate market uncertainty or guarantee profitable outcomes.

!

Ignoring Risk Limits

Decide in advance how much you can afford to lose and understand how the chosen tool handles drawdowns.

!

Trusting Unverified Performance Claims

Be cautious of claims about guaranteed income, extremely high win rates, or risk-free trading bots.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is automated trading available on Deriv?

Deriv offers Deriv Bot, a visual tool for building automated trading strategies. Availability and supported functionality can depend on the platform, product, and account.

Can beginners use Deriv Bot?

Beginners can learn how it works using official tutorials and a demo account. Understanding the strategy and its risks is important before moving to real-money trading.

Do I need programming skills to use Deriv Bot?

Deriv Bot provides a visual, block-based interface, so basic strategy building does not necessarily require coding. More advanced external integrations may have different requirements.

Can automated trading lose money?

Yes. A bot can produce losing trades, and real-money trading can result in financial losses. Automation does not remove market risk.

Should I use an external bot or Deriv Bot?

There is no single best choice for everyone. Compare platform compatibility, supported features, transparency, security, costs, and available testing options before deciding.
