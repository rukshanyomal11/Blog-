When getting started with an all-in-one automated trading platform like FXnod, understanding how payments and balances work is essential.

Traders often ask: "Does my trading money go into FXnod, or does it stay in Deriv? How do I pay for premium tools and bot subscriptions?"

The answer is simple and completely transparent. FXnod keeps your trading capital and your software subscription balance completely separate. Your trading money stays 100% safe inside your Deriv account, while FXnod features a dedicated Internal Wallet designed specifically for managing platform access, unlocking premium bot tools, and handling balance transfers.

How to Fund Your FXnod Wallet (Step-by-Step)

Adding funds to your FXnod wallet is fast, safe, and straightforward:

1

Navigate to the Wallet Tab

Log in to your FXnod dashboard on your computer or open the mobile app. From the primary navigation bar or side menu, click or tap on Wallet.

2

Review Your Current Available Balance

At the top of the wallet screen, you will see your Available Balance displayed clearly in USD, along with quick buttons for Deposit and Transfer.

3

Choose Your Deposit Method

Click on Deposit / Top Up. FXnod supports modern, low-fee digital funding options (such as USDT / Crypto networks):

  1. Select your preferred currency network.
  2. The platform will display a unique deposit address and a scannable QR code.
  3. Send the desired amount from your personal wallet or exchange.
4

Instant Confirmation & Balance Update

Once the blockchain network confirms the transaction, your FXnod wallet balance updates automatically. You will receive an immediate confirmation badge on your screen.

How to Transfer Balance Between Accounts

FXnod includes a convenient Transfer feature. This allows users to send internal wallet funds to friends, trading partners, or secondary profiles without third-party processing fees:

  1. In the Wallet section, tap the Transfer tab.
  2. Enter the recipient’s registered FXnod Email or User ID.
  3. Enter the amount you wish to send in USD.
  4. Review the confirmation summary (recipient name, amount, fee: $0.00).
  5. Tap Send Transfer. The recipient receives the funds instantly in their FXnod wallet!

Transparency is central to FXnod. Every payment or transfer generates a permanent digital receipt:

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I withdraw money from my FXnod Wallet back to my bank?

The FXnod internal wallet is designed specifically as a software billing wallet for unlocking platform tools and subscriptions. To manage or withdraw your trading profits, you do so directly through your official Deriv account cashier.

What happens if my tool subscription expires?

If your 1-month or 3-month subscription period ends, the tool status will change to Expired. Your historical trade data and settings are safely preserved. You can simply renew the tool from your wallet balance whenever you wish.

Are there any fees for transferring balance to another FXnod user?

No. Internal peer-to-peer balance transfers between FXnod accounts are instant and free of processing charges.

Can I run bots if my FXnod wallet balance is $0?

Yes! You can always test all 22 Auto Hub bots on your Deriv Virtual Demo account for free, even with a $0 FXnod wallet balance. You only need wallet funds when purchasing premium commercial tool passes.

Is my wallet information secure on my phone?

Yes. If you enable the built-in App Lock on the FXnod Mobile App, no one can access your wallet or transfer funds without your Face ID, Fingerprint, or 4-digit security PIN.
