The 4 Key Elements of the Sheet Explained

When you tap "View Loss Run →" on any bot in the Auto Hub, the sheet presents four transparent data points:

🔢 1. Step (Loss Count)

Indicates the trade number in an unbroken losing streak (#1, #2, #3, #4...). Remember that in live trading, any winning trade immediately resets this counter back to Step 1. The sheet specifically models the worst-case streak.

💵 2. Stake (Trade Size)

Shows the exact dollar amount that the bot will place on this specific trade. Notice how the stake scales based on the plan’s calibrated recovery multiplier (e.g., $1.00 → $2.17 → $4.71).

📉 3. Cumulative Loss

Shows the total amount lost across the entire streak up to that point. For example, by Step 3, your total session drawdown is -$7.88.

🛡️ 4. The Refused Stake

Look at Step #5 in the table above: total lost so far is -$18.10. If the bot placed the next planned stake of $22.18, a loss would push drawdown to -$40.28—violating your $20 Stop-Loss. FXnod's engine automatically refuses to place the trade! The bot halts safely, locking in your remaining balance. Learn more: The Ultimate Guide to Safe Martingale.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does the Loss Progression Sheet show what will definitely happen?

No. The sheet illustrates your worst-case scenario (an unbroken losing streak). In real trading, most trades win, and every winning trade resets the sequence back to Step 1. The sheet simply shows you your safety boundaries.

What does "Refused Stake" mean?

"Refused Stake" is an automated safety rule. It means the engine calculated that placing the next planned trade would violate your pre-set Stop-Loss ceiling. To protect your capital, the bot refuses the trade and halts the session safely.

Can I see the Loss Progression Sheet for custom "Own Amount" bots?

Yes! When you configure a custom starting stake and stop-loss on an Own Amount bot, FXnod dynamically calculates and updates the Loss Progression table to reflect your custom numbers.

Is the Loss Progression Sheet available on the mobile app?

Yes! The FXnod Mobile App features an interactive modal bottom sheet that opens instantly when you tap "View Loss Run →" on any bot card. Learn more: FXnod Mobile App Guide.

What should I do if a bot hits its Stop-Loss limit?

Accept the result and take a break. The Stop-Loss protected the rest of your balance. Review your journal, wait for calm market conditions, and resume trading during your next planned session.
